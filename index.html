Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? I've got answers.

What is "vibe coding"?
Vibe coding is my approach to building websites and platforms with speed, creativity, and AI assistance. I listen to your vision and bring it to life with clean, custom code that works beautifully.
How long does a project take?
Basic websites take 3-5 days. Business websites take 5-7 days. Custom platforms take 1-3 weeks depending on features. I'll give you a timeline during consultation.
Do you offer ongoing support?
Yes! I offer maintenance plans starting at ₵20/month or $3/month to keep your site secure, updated, and running smoothly.
What platforms do you build on?
I build custom websites and platforms from the ground up using clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I also work with Firebase, Node.js, and other modern technologies.
What is the process like?
I start with a consultation to understand your vision, then I design, build, and launch your site. I involve you at every step to ensure the result matches your vision.
Do you use AI?
Yes! I use AI tools like Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and ChatGPT to build faster and more efficiently. This allows me to deliver quality work at affordable prices.
